# CargoSphere Operations Platform
# Stakeholder Analysis

**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 21 September 2026  
**Status:** Draft – Requirements Discovery

---

## 1. Purpose

This document identifies the key stakeholders involved in the CargoSphere Operations Platform and describes their responsibilities, interaction with the system, information needs and expected outcomes.

The stakeholder analysis provides the foundation for identifying user requirements and system functionality.

---

## 2. Stakeholder Classification

Stakeholders are divided into:

- Internal system users
- External system users
- External business stakeholders

Not every external stakeholder will necessarily require a direct system account.

---

## 3. Internal System Users

### 3.1 System Administrator

**Responsibility:**

- User account administration
- Role assignment
- Account activation/deactivation
- System configuration
- Reference data management
- Audit monitoring

**System needs:**

- User management
- Role management
- Account status management
- Configuration management
- Audit logs

**Expected outcome:**

A controlled and secure system environment.

---

### 3.2 Operations Manager

**Responsibility:**

- Overall operational supervision
- Shipment monitoring
- Staff/task assignment
- Exception management
- Operational performance monitoring
- Reporting

**System needs:**

- Operational dashboard
- Shipment visibility
- Search and filtering
- Exception management
- Task management
- Reports and analytics

**Expected outcome:**

Improved visibility and control over cargo operations.

---

### 3.3 Operations Officer

**Responsibility:**

- Day-to-day shipment management
- Shipment creation
- Shipment information maintenance
- Status updates
- Operational activity recording
- Issue escalation

**System needs:**

- Shipment management
- Cargo information
- Status management
- Task management
- Activity history
- Search and filtering

**Expected outcome:**

Efficient and accurate management of shipment operations.

---

### 3.4 Documentation Officer

**Responsibility:**

- Shipment documentation
- Document verification
- Missing-document identification
- Document status management

**System needs:**

- Document checklist
- Document upload
- Document verification
- Document version history
- Missing-document alerts

**Expected outcome:**

Improved document completeness and visibility.

---

### 3.5 Clearance Officer

**Responsibility:**

- Customs/clearance activities
- Clearance status management
- Clearance issue management
- Cargo release recording

**System needs:**

- Clearance records
- Clearance status
- Clearance references
- Issue tracking
- Release recording

**Expected outcome:**

Clear visibility of clearance progress and cargo release status.

---

### 3.6 Delivery Coordinator

**Responsibility:**

- Delivery coordination
- Delivery scheduling
- Transport assignment
- Delivery status management
- Proof-of-delivery management

**System needs:**

- Delivery requests
- Scheduling
- Transport assignment
- Delivery tracking
- Proof-of-delivery records

**Expected outcome:**

Organized and traceable delivery operations.

---

## 4. External System Users

### 4.1 Customer

**Responsibility:**

- Requesting and receiving cargo services
- Monitoring shipment progress
- Providing required information/documents

**System needs:**

- Shipment tracking
- Shipment information
- Customer-accessible documents
- Delivery information
- Notifications
- Account management

**Expected outcome:**

Improved shipment visibility and reduced dependency on manual status inquiries.

---

## 5. External Business Stakeholders

The following stakeholders may provide or receive information without necessarily having direct user accounts in the initial system:

### Airline Partners

Provide relevant flight and cargo information and participate in airline handover processes.

### Transport Providers

Provide transportation services for applicable deliveries.

### Customs/Clearance Partners

Participate in clearance activities and provide relevant clearance information.

### Warehouse/Cargo Handling Partners

Participate in cargo acceptance, handling and release activities.

---

## 6. Stakeholder Access Principle

Access to system information shall be based on the responsibilities of each user.

Users should only receive the access required to perform their responsibilities.

For example:

- Documentation Officers manage documentation.
- Clearance Officers manage clearance activities.
- Delivery Coordinators manage delivery operations.
- Customers access their own shipment information.
- System Administrators manage system access and configuration.

## 8. Key Stakeholder Requirements

The stakeholder analysis identified the following major business needs:

1. Centralized shipment information.
2. Role-specific system access.
3. Shipment lifecycle visibility.
4. Documentation visibility.
5. Clearance status visibility.
6. Delivery tracking.
7. Exception management.
8. Customer shipment visibility.
9. Operational reporting.
10. Historical activity and auditability.
